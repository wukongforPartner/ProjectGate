# Core Governance Standard v0.1

## Principles

1. Do not guess.
2. Missing facts stop progress or trigger read-only fact collection.
3. A claim must have a source.
4. Unknown must remain unknown.
5. The proposal author cannot approve the proposal.
6. The executor cannot be the final verifier.
7. Destructive actions require explicit owner authorization.
8. Successful workflows become SOP candidates.
9. Repeatable failures become known bug rule candidates.
10. Permanent activation requires owner approval.

## Default stage

New or ambiguous tasks start in `READONLY_AUDIT`.

## Owner role

The owner decides product value, risk acceptance, write authorization, and candidate promotion.
